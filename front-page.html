1Strait of Hormuz · Energy · Iran war

Three tankers strike mines in Hormuz as Iran defies the US blockadeConfirmed

Iran said three oil tankers hit mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, the waterway that carries a fifth of the world’s oil, and Tehran refused talks with Washington until its seven conditions are met. Foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei called the US naval blockade, reimposed in July, a military aggression, and said Iran would use “any available tool” to confront it. Vice-President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio met Qatar’s prime minister separately at the White House. Sources: AP · Reuters

2Technology · Markets

Huang restored at Nvidia; Nasdaq leaps 6.51% and Asia’s chip shares soarConfirmed

Nvidia’s board unanimously reinstated founder Jensen Huang as chief executive, and President Trump said he personally persuaded the board. Asia followed on Tuesday: Taiwan’s Taiex rose ~9% (TSMC up nearly 10%) and Seoul’s Kospi gained over 6% through 5,000. SEC staff are also investigating accounting questions at the chipmaker, Reuters reported. Sources: Reuters · AP

3Russia–Ukraine war

Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia flats kills two and wounds 40Confirmed

An overnight Russian attack struck an apartment building in the city of Zaporizhzhia, killing two people and wounding 40, including four children, regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said. In Berlin, Germany became the first country granted access to Ukraine’s Delta battlefield-management system. Sources: Reuters · AP

4Gaza

Israeli strike kills Hamas’s security chief in GazaConfirmed

Israeli airstrikes killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas’s internal security apparatus, and six others, including two police officers, medics and police said. Monday’s strikes had already killed at least three people in Gaza, one of them a worker for the UN Palestinian refugee agency. Sources: Reuters · Al Jazeera

5Libya · United Nations

UN demands an inquiry into the Benghazi port attackConfirmed

The UN Support Mission in Libya called for an independent investigation into Sunday’s attacks at Benghazi port and on a Russian warship off the eastern coast, as Kyiv explained its strike in Libya. Sources: UN News · Reuters

6NATO · Space

Rutte: NATO will defend space after Russian nuclear-satellite reportsReported

NATO chief Mark Rutte said the alliance will defend against any threats in space, after reports that Russia is preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite. Western intelligence, via German media, puts a launch within weeks. Sources: BBC · German media

7Congo · Ebola

Fighting traps Ebola teams near an Ituri hospitalConfirmed

Heavy fighting near a hospital in Ituri province, DR Congo, disrupted Ebola response work and forced emergency teams to take cover for over 24 hours, the WHO said. Sources: UN News · WHO

8Britain · Security

Britain warns Ukraine’s suppliers to brace for Russian sabotageConfirmed

Britain’s defence ministry and MI5 issued a rare public alert: companies helping Ukraine’s military face a heightened Russian sabotage risk and should harden sites and supply chains now. Sources: Reuters · AP
